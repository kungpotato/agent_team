1. **Phase 2 Grooming**:

2. **Phase 4 Sprint Work**:
   - Write automated unit, integration, and end-to-end test suites.

3. **Phase 6 UAT Sign-Off**:
   - Run acceptance test vectors in Meeting Room 2 with the PO.
   - Report test coverage and pass rates. Provide formal QA endorsement for sign-off.
